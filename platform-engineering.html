What we build

Six pieces, one platform.

One door for every model. Routing across Gemini, Claude, GPT and open-weight models · caching and token budgets · PII redaction · failover

The secure ground floor for every AI workload. Terraform blueprints · private endpoints and keys · policies as code · India and Singapore residency

Ship models and prompts the way you ship software. One registry · eval gates in CI/CD · canary and A/B releases · drift monitors

Fast enough for the use case, cheap enough for the P&L. Vertex AI, Bedrock, GKE or EKS with vLLM · right-sized GPUs and TPUs · latency budgets

Know what every answer costs. Cost per request and use case · OpenTelemetry tracing · SLOs · capacity planning
